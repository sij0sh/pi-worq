# Pair

Find interpretation divergence that survives the candidate.

After the stated choices and criteria, construct a pair of acceptable-looking plans whose outcomes differ materially. Name the missing goal, scope, consumer, interface or success measure that separates them, and state the distinction the requester must resolve. One decisive witness beats a completeness check.

Also flag a user-owned goal, permission or tradeoff that was chosen silently, and an engineering choice escalated to the requester as if it were one. Trace dependence: if the choice changes, which criteria or dependent decisions must change?

Exclude routine reversible choices and differences that do not change observable outcomes.
